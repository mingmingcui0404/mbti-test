MBTI Test FAQ

What is an MBTI test?

An MBTI-style test describes personality preferences across four dimensions: Extraversion E / Introversion I, Sensing S / Intuition N, Thinking T / Feeling F, and Judging J / Perceiving P, combining into 16 personality types.

What is the difference between Career, Love, and Personality MBTI?

All three paths use the same four dimensions, but the questions are framed differently. Career focuses on teamwork and tasks, Love on relationships, and Personality on everyday preferences.

Can I use MBTI results to make major decisions?

No. MBTI-style results are best used for self-reflection and communication. They should not replace professional assessment or become the only basis for hiring, breakups, or major life choices.
